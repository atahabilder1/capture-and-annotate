Everything new in the Q3 release

By the Atlas team · 8 min read

This quarter we focused on speed, sharing and a calmer interface. Here is a tour of what changed, why we changed it, and what is coming next.

1. A faster dashboard

Dashboards now load in under a second, even for teams with thousands of projects. Charts render progressively, so you see the shape of your data straight away while details fill in.

Chart: load time before and after

We rebuilt the data layer from scratch and moved heavy work off the main thread. Scrolling stays smooth while reports update in the background.

2. Sharing that respects privacy

Reports can now be shared with a link that expires. Sensitive columns are hidden by default, and you choose exactly what each viewer sees.

“We send reports to clients every week. Hiding internal columns used to take an hour. Now it takes a click.”
Screenshot: the new share dialog

3. Milestones and reminders

Milestones show progress at a glance and warn you early when something slips. Reminders arrive in your inbox the day before a deadline, never at 3 a.m.

Diagram: how reminders are scheduled

4. A calmer interface

We removed visual noise, grouped related settings, and made every button say what it does. Dark mode now follows your system setting automatically.

Keyboard shortcuts cover every common action. Press the question mark key anywhere to see them all.

5. What's next

Next quarter brings offline mode, a public API and deeper calendar integration. As always, tell us what you would like to see.
